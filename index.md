## What is Rfxcom2mqtt?

Rfxcom2mqtt is a bridge that connects your RFXCOM transceiver to your MQTT broker, allowing you to integrate various RF devices (like remote controls, sensors, and switches) into your smart home system. It supports a wide range of protocols and device types, making it a versatile solution for home automation.

## Key Benefits

- **Protocol Translation**: Converts RF signals to MQTT messages and vice versa
- **Device Management**: Discover, pair, and manage your RF devices through a simple interface
- **Automation Ready**: Easily integrate with automation platforms like Home Assistant, Node-RED, and more
- **Extensible**: Support for various RFXCOM protocols and device types
